Best Energy Company (BEC)

Best Energy Company (BEC) was established in 2000 under Makana Holding Company, in Saudi Arabia. BEC has diversified business interests in industrial operations & maintenance, industrial services, and procurement sectors. Best Energy Company has offices in Eastern & Western provinces of Saudi Arabia, and successfully inaugurated a unique branch in Riyadh, reflecting the company’s business values, partnerships and solid expansion capability obtained.
